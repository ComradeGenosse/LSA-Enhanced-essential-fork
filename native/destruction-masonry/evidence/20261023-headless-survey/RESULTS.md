## 3. X9 asset specification (for the custom-wall path)

Because no genuine source exists, X9 should follow X8's proven pattern (a lab-authored fence staged from a lab YMAP in the lab DLC) rather than hide a stock prop.

- **Envelope:** width 3.0 m, height 2.0 m (2.0–2.2 acceptable), thickness 0.25 m; local origin at ground centre, +Y thickness axis, +X along the wall. Five 0.6 m columns → sections `0..4` at local X `[-1.5,-0.9] … [0.9,1.5]`; the centre pair (sections 1–2 or 2–3 per X7 pair selection) opens 1.2 m ≥ 1 m. See [`synthetic-target-plan.json`](synthetic-target-plan.json) (generated by `recipe.py --fixture`; status `OFFLINE_FIXTURE_NOT_DEPLOYABLE`, plan SHA-256 `05ec2eed53f2…`). It is a design target, **not** attested qualification.
- **Indices:** keep physical indices 0..4 so X7/X8's validated impact selector is unchanged.
- **Needed new assets (author locally, never redistribute):** one fragment root per section (5), brick/concrete material, per-section collision (box or convex per column), a closed-wall parent frag/bounds, an LOD (or none with lodDist ≥ 100), a `.ytyp` with unique archetype name, and a lab `.ymap` at a cleared site; GEN9 conversion for YFT/YTD per the CodeWalker 30 dev48 ShadersGen9Conversion pipeline already used for `lsa_x2_concrete`.
- **Reusable unchanged from X8:** impact selector, native staging/lease, section witnesses and 9/9 restore logic, cleanup, P2 ownership/loader. **New:** a masonry family adapter (never pass a brick/concrete hash through X8's fence-only `X8Catalog` / `lsa_x7d_secp_j1400`).
