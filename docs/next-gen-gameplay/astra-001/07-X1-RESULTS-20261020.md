# ASTRA-001 X1: native fragment destruction results

## Verdict

**PASS for existence proof: native fragment breakage, collision removal, and automated ped traversal through a gap in a five-panel fence barrier.** One of two debris-present explosion runs met the strict direct-walk criterion; the second crossed but exceeded the lateral-deviation threshold and is explicitly unverified. Existing RAGE fragments are a viable basis for a coarse destructible barrier prototype on this tested GTA Enhanced installation; repeatable direct traversal is not yet accepted.

**PARTIAL SUCCESS against the wider wall objective:** the successful structure uses adjacent vanilla corrugated-metal fence props. The experiment does not demonstrate a local hole inside one concrete wall, an attended player-thrown explosive and player traversal session, or arbitrary vanilla building destruction. Those distinctions remain acceptance boundaries, not inferred successes.

## T2/T3: panel-line collision and traversal — PASS within stated scope

Five panels, 0.05 m inter-panel gap, static variant (unfrozen, entity dynamic flag disabled), 0.25 m ray-grid pitch. A native grenade is added 0.4 m in front of panel 2 at 45% of panel height, damage scale 1. Each panel has 169 baseline root-blocked ray cells out of 171 grid cells. The player is temporarily moved to a flat airport patch; only disposable lab-owned peds are tasked.

The original source run `20261020-224902-f5d207` returned:

| Case | Clear/root-removed fractions by panel | Traversal | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| No explosion | 0, 0, 0, 0, 0 | Intact control blocked | `CONTROL_STAYED_CLOSED` |
| Grenade | 0, 0.48, 1, 1, 0 | Hole crossed, control blocked | `GAP_IN_WALL_CONFIRMED` |

**Limitation of that first run:** Claude's original line test deleted detected debris before measuring and walking. It proves a cleanup-assisted opening, not debris-present traversability.

X1-0.2 run `20261020-225019-8328fd` repeats the control and grenade twice with `cleardebris=0`. Opening fractions now require completely clear rays, not merely absence of the original root. Swept spheres use radius 0.3 m and four vertical samples per lateral position; unresolved requests count as blocked. Walking records lateral deviation and rejects off-lane or climbing crossings.

The first debris-present grenade trial produced fractions `0, 0.39, 1, 0.98, 0`. Panel 2: 169/169 previously blocked rays clear, zero residual cells, 76/76 sphere sweeps clear across a 4.75 m window. The hole ped crossed in 2669 ms, zero vertical gain, maximum lateral deviation 0.007 m. The intact control blocked. Two outer panels remained closed. Debris remained during these checks; 13 nearby debris candidates were recorded. Root movement and debris can vary, so this is a coarse native panel breach rather than controlled concrete fracture geometry.

The second debris-present grenade trial produced fractions `0, 0.48, 1, 0.96, 0`. The hole ped crossed in 2956 ms with zero vertical gain, but maximum lateral deviation was **0.654 m**, above the fixed 0.6 m bound. Its verdict is **`GAP_PARTIAL_UNVERIFIED_WALK`**, not a traversal PASS. The intact control blocked; 16 debris candidates remained. Both grenade runs cleared the center-panel rays and capsule window, but only **1/2** passed the strict direct-walk check. We did not relax the threshold or discard the unfavorable repeat. The record does not establish whether debris avoidance, installed-mod interference, or task steering caused that deviation.

## Practical limits and next experiment

- **Code compiled:** yes, offline compiler against the actual installed API, with existing obsolete/unused warnings.
- **Code loaded:** yes, fresh SHVDN log and runtime source SHA.
- **Behavior demonstrated in GTA:** native detached fragments in inherited raw evidence, then current line collision and ped-walk evidence. No synthetic test is being substituted for gameplay.
- Existing ASI/SHVDN mods remained installed. RAGE Plugin Hook was not started. Police/wanted overlays were visible despite the lab's wanted-suppression calls; this is an installed-stack experiment, not clean vanilla calibration. No mod settings were changed to suppress that behavior.
- The script restores its player position and invincibility and cleans owned entities after each batch. It suppresses wanted level during trials but does not restore a previous nonzero wanted level. Do not use it during a mission or a valuable active pursuit.
- The reported connected-component width/height is a bounding box, not proof that every enclosed point is empty. The swept spheres and actual ped passage supply the additional evidence.
- No NVE replacement audit was performed in this continuation. Conclusions apply to this installed model/runtime combination.

Smallest remaining acceptance step: an attended player throw-and-walk check of the same panel barrier. For material-specific local holes and predictable break shapes, X2's custom fragment wall is the next asset experiment; it was not implemented here. X4, persistence, collapse, NPC pathfinding integration, production integration, and city-scale claims remain out of scope.
