# ASTRA-001 X4: first destructible Los Santos wall

Date 2026-10-21. Offline Story Mode. Base: X3 `d16a4e9c`. Draft, unmerged. X2/X3 evidence untouched.

## Result

One vanilla Los Santos wall was replaced by the native-fragment wall, blown apart with a real RPG projectile, and walked through by the PLAYER, in the live game.

- Target: `prop_fnccorgm_02e` (corrugated metal fence, `v_fences.rpf`, hash 1185366416) at 1065.758, -1773.716, 34.851, Elysian Island, upright. A free-standing fence on a corner lot with open ground behind it. Not overridden by the NVE, forest or LA Roads packs.
- Original removed, including collision: `CREATE_MODEL_HIDE_EXCLUDING_SCRIPT_OBJECTS` (radius 0.15) on that single prop. Before the hide a 360-cell ray grid hit the wall in 360/360 cells; after it, 0/360 cells and 7/7 full-height sweeps were clear (zero residual collision). Neighbour fence segments were unaffected after the hide (24/24 neighbour rays unchanged). No ybn excision was needed. The hide is runtime-only and reverts with `REMOVE_MODEL_HIDE` or on game exit, so no game file is touched.
- Replacement: frozen X2 GEN9 `lsa_x2_concrete` (30 chunks), spawned at the original's measured footprint centre and heading (110.19 degrees), same native fixed-joint anchor as X2/X3.
- Rocket: real `SHOOT_SINGLE_BULLET_BETWEEN_COORDS` RPG projectile from about 8 m. The native-explosion fallback did not trigger. 16 native fragments detached; root moved 0.025 m; 20/20 anchor-face hits.
- Opening: 47 of the 64 wall cells measured by the grid became clear, with a 1.5 m wide by 1.75 m tall clear block starting at the floor, plus a lintel above. No full-height sweep is clear because of the lintel, so the pass criterion is the walk itself.
- Player walk: the PLAYER (`isPlayer` true) was tasked from 2 m in front of the wall and crossed the wall plane at u = 0.244 m from centre, inside the original footprint, no rise (maxRise 0), 5.3 s, no detour (`x4-evidence/x4-final-run.jsonl`, `x4_player_result`).

Screenshots were taken in game (intact wall, projectile in flight, doorway after the blast, player inside the opening) and are kept locally with the telemetry; images are not committed.

## What is not clean

- The replacement is a plain concrete slab, not corrugated metal, so up close it does not look like the original fence. X4 proves mechanism, not art. A matching texture is the obvious next step.
- The asset is 3.0 m wide against the original's 2.54 m, so it overhangs about 0.23 m onto the neighbouring fence at each end. I trimmed the measured footprint to the original's width for the probes; the overhang is visible.
- The `x4_neighbours` check reports `unchanged: false` after the replacement spawns and after restore. After spawn that is the overhang. After restore the raw native rays return garbage, so I treat it as unreliable, not as damage. The restore ray grid hit the wall in 353/360 cells where the original showed 360/360; the final runs use the SHVDN raycast, which ignores see-through parts, so this is most likely the probe method and not an incomplete restore. I did not re-verify the restore visually.
- In the first full test the RPG blast also destroyed a car parked beside the fence and traffic and pedestrians reacted; that is ordinary sandbox behaviour, not lab cleanup, and nothing of it was deleted by the lab.

## Crash investigation (cost most of the time)

GTA crashed on four runs a few frames after the replacement was probed. Ruled out: the model hide, the gameplay-camera setters, and spawning (the wall idled 10 s fine with probes disabled). Cause: the raw `START_EXPENSIVE_SYNCHRONOUS_SHAPE_TEST_LOS_PROBE(..., flags 17, 0, 0)` call crashes in the city when it hits the fragment wall; X3's desert tests never hit it. SHVDN `World.Raycast` with the same flags is stable, so X4 grid probes use it. I also fixed a real bug on the way: the wall was being rotated after spawn while its anchor stayed at heading 0; wall and anchor are now created at the target heading. Raw crash telemetry (the four crashed runs, concatenated): `x4-evidence/crash-runs.jsonl`.

## Install state

All temporary X4 files were removed. The INI, X2 script, DLC list and pack hashes were verified against the pre-test values. GTA was closed after testing.
