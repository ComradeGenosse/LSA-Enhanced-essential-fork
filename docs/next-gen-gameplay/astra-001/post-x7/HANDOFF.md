   No per-frame city scan; inspect the two placements. Preparation is outside

   unready collision, pool pressure and stale instances. Unsupported approach
   leaves original in charge and records a refusal; no fallback explosion.

   its trigger restrictions and physical index convention. Later hits on an
   Active object do not regenerate it; widening remains out of scope.
5. Implement instance-scoped retirement, failure reconciliation and restore.
   A cannot mutate B. After success/miss, unhide/reacquire and require 9/9 plus
   visible original. If partial failure, retain state, stop commands and expose
   RestorePending rather than claiming None. Ordinary retirement deliberately
   resets session damage; no persistence claim.
6. Add correlated evidence fields: instance/definition revision, candidate/runtime
   identity, source transform, stage/commit frame, selection, owned roots/anchors,
